Homemade Pancake Mix

  • Home

Everyone enjoys pancakes.

WHY YOU WILL LOVE THIS RECIPE
  • Basic ingredients—flour, powdered milk, and sugar—make up this homemade pancake mix in just minutes.
  • Home cooks like the recipe's flexibility to cater to allergies, make extra-fluffy pancakes, or add-ins.
  • Odinrecipes member Yoly raves, "We loved this! Pancakes came out light and fluffy."

Ingredients

  • 5 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 ¼ cups powdered milk
  • ¼ cup white sugar
  • ¼ cup baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon salt

Steps

Step 1

Combine flour, powdered milk, sugar, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl; stir to blend. Store mixture in an airtight container and use within 8 months.

To make Pancake Batter

  • 2 cups pancake mix
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons oil (for waffles, use 3 tablespoons)

Beat together pancake mix, water, egg, and oil in a large bowl with a wire whisk or fork until smooth. This makes eight 4-inch pancakes or 4 large waffles.
